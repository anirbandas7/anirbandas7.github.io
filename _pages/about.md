I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Information Systems at [BITS Pilani, K K Birla Goa Campus](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/goa/anirban-das/). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the [Indian Institute of Information Technology Guwahati](https://www.iiitg.ac.in), and was then a Post-Doctoral Fellow in the [Ubiquitous Networked Systems Lab (UbiNet)](https://cse.iitkgp.ac.in/resgrp/ubinet/) at [IIT Kharagpur](http://www.iitkgp.ac.in).

My current work spans human-computer interaction, the Internet of Things, acoustic sensing and wireless (mmWave and Wi-Fi) sensing: passive driver monitoring with mmWave radar, multi-user activity tracking, contactless music generation from ambient wireless signals, and on-road context assessment for Indian traffic. Earlier, I worked on in-network data fusion for resource-constrained environments, machine inference on low-power and embedded devices, sentiment analysis on large-scale text, and community detection in social networks.

I have over six years of teaching experience in India and abroad. I am always open to discussions, collaborations and research opportunities, so feel free to get in touch by email.
